I build AI-assisted compliance tools for licensed firms in the Bailiwick of Guernsey. FSR is the first of these — a single repository of the regulations your firm is required to comply with, fully cross-referenced across rules, guidance notes, definitions and statutory provisions.

FSR was built for Bank Aston Limited and covers the regulatory documents identified as critical obligations for a licensed bank — 9,079 entries in total. Each entry preserves the verbatim source text and carries full metadata — so a compliance team can find any rule, understand its context, and evidence their compliance, without cross-referencing multiple PDF documents.

If your firm is regulated in the Bailiwick and you're spending time hunting through regulatory documents, get in touch. The conversation is free.
